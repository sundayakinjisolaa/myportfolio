🚕 NYC Yellow Taxi Commercial Analytics

Commercial Analytics · Python · DuckDB · SQL · Power BI

Analysed more than 75 million real NYC Yellow Taxi trips from January 2025 to July 2026 to understand demand, revenue, geographic performance and operational patterns.

75M+ Trips analysed
$791M Jan–Jul 2026 revenue
+7.4% Revenue vs matched 2025
+11.9% Revenue per trip

Key finding: Revenue increased while trip volume declined in the matched January–July period, indicating higher observed revenue per trip rather than growth being driven simply by more trips.
